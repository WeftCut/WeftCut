## Upgrading from 0.8.0

**macOS: install this version once from the DMG.** WeftCut 0.8.0 cannot update
itself on macOS. Download `WeftCut-mac-arm64.dmg` below and drag WeftCut into
Applications, replacing the old copy. From this version on it updates itself
like Windows and Linux. Keep it in Applications: a copy opened from the disk
image or from Downloads says it cannot update there.

Windows and Linux update to this version as usual. Projects are unchanged: 0.8.0
and 0.8.1 open each other's projects.

## What's new

- **macOS updates itself**: new versions download in the background and install
  when you quit, or right away with **Update and restart**. On a Mac an update
  that has finished downloading installs at the next quit, even if you then turn
  automatic install off.
- **Reviewing an agent's changes** (agent view, **Changes** tab):
  - **Changed ranges** show where the film plays differently, on the ruler and
    the mini timeline. A clip that only slid along is not a change.
  - A range that is only **out of sync** says how far picture and sound drifted,
    and stays quiet unless linked sync broke.
  - Double-click a range on the ruler to review it as a whole. **Alt+↑/↓** walk
    the ranges, **Shift+Space** plays the focused one.
  - The **range bar** shows how many ranges the timeline has and how long they
    run; the tab opens with a count of the unreviewed changes.
  - The agent view always shows the whole film, whichever Group the editor has
    open.
- **Fixes**: a cancelled export reads as cancelled at every stage and in the
  log; a short clip stays inside its own span at every zoom; the mini timeline's
  pane fits its rows.
